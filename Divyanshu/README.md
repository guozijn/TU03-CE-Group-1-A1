## About the Project

This project looks at LinkedIn job postings collected in 2024. Our main aim was to explore the job market for people with Business Analyst qualifications and skills.

For my part of the analysis, I focused on two main questions:

- Which job titles are related to Business Analyst roles?
- Where are these jobs located?

I also looked at some data quality issues in the dataset, such as missing values, and considered how these issues could affect the analysis.

## What I Used

I used Tableau Public to clean and prepare the data, explore the dataset and create the visualisations used in the analysis.

## My Analysis

The Tableau workbook includes:

- An overview of the most common job titles
- Business Analyst-related job titles
- Job postings by country
- Data quality checks and missing-value analysis

These visualisations helped me get an initial understanding of the job market and narrow down the focus of the project.
